Each direction shows the logged-out desktop page and, on the right, the phone while you talk to the agent, with the restaurant card found from the link. The copy is the same everywhere; only the character changes. Pick one, or a mix (the colour of one with the orb of another).

A · Il bigliettino

the note you pass instead of speaking

The shy person does not phone: they slip a note. Powder pink paper with ink blue, like the carbon copy of an order pad. The orb is an ink stamp that sways when Shy Order speaks. The restaurant card is a slip with a perforated edge. It is kind and a little ironic, with no tech look.

the 1965 SIP Grillo telephone
